My top 5 Video Game List

This is a conclusive list of my top 5 Video Games ranked top to bottom from my most liked, decending to "lesser" titles.

  1. Batman Arkham Series

    The Batman Arkham Series is my favorite trilogy of games. It follows Batman and his victories over the classic forces of evil. I love this series to bits, as it contains some of the best game machanics I have ever played along with a stellar story.

  2. Portal 2

    The game Portal 2, has a special place in my heart. It is one of the best story driven puzzle games I have ever played. It follows Chell, The protagonist through a maze of testing facilities with the singular goal of escaping the laboritory known as Apature Science.

  3. Skyrim: Elder Scrolls

    Skyrim is my favorite RPG, and has maintained this for over 9 years now. It Has the mystical world of the Elder Scrolls combined with the great, unique story of the Skyrim portion of the overall world. I always end up finding out about a new adventure to be had each playthrough.

  4. Astroneer

    This game is a unique resource managment game with deeply cathardic gameplay. With every resource gathered there is always a chance of either great improvment, or massive loss as you realize you have traveld to far from home base and are running out of oxygen.

  5. Cyberpunk 2077

    The Cyberpunk universe takes place in a distopia, where the main player has to navigate a complete corprate and gang takeover of the fictional Night City. Through the game you, the player, are haunted by the image of Keanu Reeves. Obviously the grity and often times hillarious nature of the story allow for hyjinx to ensue.
